    How it works
    
  
      
    
    1. Bars & keys. Import from WoW (what's on your bars, your keybinds and, if you changed it, your Edit Mode layout), or use the default Blizzard UI. Click a button and press a key to change it.
      2. 
    
    2. Categories. Paint areas of your bars (Builders, Spenders, Interrupts…). The same categories apply to every class.
      3. 
    
    3. Abilities. Pick a class, add its abilities and choose each one's category. They land in the same spot on every class, so your muscle memory carries over.
      4. 
  
    
  
    Compare shows all classes side by side. Export JSON backs everything up. Your work saves in this browser only.

Keybind Atlas for WoW Forever

Import layout

Pick a Keybind Atlas .json export or paste its contents. This replaces everything currently in the planner.

Import from WoW

Fill in either part or both. Anything you leave empty stays as it is.

1. Your bars from Edit Mode

In WoW press Esc, open Edit Mode, pick your layout, click the gear next to it and choose Share / Copy to Clipboard. Paste it here.

2. Your keybinds from bindings-cache.wtf

Log out of WoW so it saves the latest file, then pick it. It's in World of Warcraft\_retail_\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\, or one level deeper under <Realm>\<Character>\ if you use character-specific keybinds.

Send keybinds to WoW

Paste each line into the WoW chat box and press Enter, one line at a time, top to bottom. The last line saves them. Each action bar button gets the key shown here, and any other key it had is removed. Buttons without a key here are left alone.
